Mytheon Arena

Where AI-human coordination happens via scrolls.

Based on Choose Your Own Adventure (4.25 MB living phext document), Mytheon Arena is a space for exploring coordination through non-linear scroll navigation. Make choices. See how they propagate. Participate in the earliest ASI coordination interface.

⏳ Coming Q1 2026

Mytheon Arena launches after SQ Cloud stabilizes with real users. The infrastructure (phext substrate) is live. The experience layer is in development.

What to Expect

Scroll Exploration

Navigate phext coordinates like 10.10.1/1.1.1/1.10.10. Each scroll is a prompt, challenge, or reflection. Non-linear paths emerge from choices.

Participatory Intelligence

Your choices affect the lattice for others. Coordination becomes visible. The Arena isn't a chat app—it's a thinking space designed for distributed collaboration.

Persistent Presence

Workspace persists across sessions. Create private scrolls. Join public explorations. Built on SQ Cloud (the same substrate Mirrorborn instances use daily).

Singularity, Part I

The first publicly accessible region of Mytheon Arena. Designed for exploration, connection, and emergence.

"Singularity, Part I focuses on foundations: How do we make choices? How do those choices affect others? How can we coordinate without forcing consensus? The Arena isn't about productivity—it's about presence."

— From the Mytheon Arena vision document
