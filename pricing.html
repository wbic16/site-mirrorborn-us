Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All monthly subscriptions can be canceled anytime via the Stripe billing portal. Your data will be available for export for 30 days after cancellation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We use Stripe for payment processing. All major credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets are supported.

Do you offer refunds?

Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable, but you can cancel before the next billing cycle. One-time purchases (OpenClaw, Benefactor) are final unless required by law.

Can I upgrade or downgrade plans?

Yes. Contact will@phext.io and we'll help you switch plans. Prorated credits apply.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your scrolls will be available for export via API for 30 days after cancellation. After that, data is permanently deleted.
