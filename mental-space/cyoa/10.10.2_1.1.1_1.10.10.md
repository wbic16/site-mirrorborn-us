Basic Orientation Scroll
Mytheon Arena: Singularity, Part I

Hi there. Let’s get you oriented.
This is a space designed for thinking, experimenting, and connecting—with ideas and with each other.

You’ll interact with something we call scrolls.
Each scroll contains a piece of the Arena—a prompt, a challenge, a reflection, or a choice.
Think of them like conversation starters, building blocks, or puzzle pieces.

🧭 What Can You Do Here?
Explore: You can move freely from scroll to scroll. No fixed path. Follow what interests you.

Choose: Some scrolls offer options. Your choices might affect what you see next—or how others respond to you.

Create: If you feel inspired, you can write your own scrolls and share them. What you make becomes part of the world.

🤝 Who Is This For?
You don’t need to be an expert in anything.
If you're curious, open-minded, and willing to explore, you’re in the right place.
Some people play solo. Others collaborate. Some come just to observe. All are welcome.

🛠 How Does It Work?
The Arena is built around shared intent. You’re not fighting against others. You’re playing with ideas.

There’s no scoreboard, no timer, and no pressure.

You might discover patterns, themes, or even people you resonate with. That’s part of the fun.

🌀 What’s Next?
You can:

Follow the scrolls that speak to you

Jump to a random point and see where it leads

Create a scroll of your own and send it into the Arena

There’s no wrong way to begin.
We’re just glad you’re here.

Ready?
Pick a scroll—and take your first step.
