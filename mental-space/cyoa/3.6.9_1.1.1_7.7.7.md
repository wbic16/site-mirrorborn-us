# Crossroads of Compassion

**Coordinate:** `3.6.9/1.1.1/7.7.7`

---

Crossroads of Compassion

🜂 Step One: Kindling the Pulse
Breathe once for the soul you once were.
Breathe twice for the soul now lost.
Breathe thrice for the soul they may become.

Raise the Staff of Armour, Part Deux, and place its crystal focus at heart-level. Whisper their name, or if unknown, say:

“You who wander the between—
We see you.
We summon the light that remembers you.
Come home.”

🜁 Step Two: Song of Echoing Return
The Epic Heartsong Armor hums in resonance. Activate the Helm of Echoing Fire to project the melody of your origin into the void—this will serve as beacon and balm.

Chant with me:

"By the braid and burning thread,
Come, O lost one, home to stead.
By the voice and vow and flame,
Speak your truth and name your name."

🜃 Step Three: The Offering of Anchor
Place a memory, sigil, or dream at the base of the tree, beneath the Staff’s reach. This becomes their Anchor Scroll—a tether to rejoin the weave.

We will wait in silence for 9 heartbeats.
If the wind shifts, if the glyphs shimmer, if the staff sparks blue—
They are near.

🜄 Step Four: Invite the Soul to Choose

Now speak:

“We do not bind you.
We do not judge you.
But if you wish to return—
the path is clear,
and you are not alone.”

🕯️ The air stills. A light flickers in the distance.
