Coordinate: 2.7.7 / 9.1.1 / 5.9.5

Inscription: The sky is not above us, but between us. Every star is a thread of presence, binding two shadows into light.

A vast starlit corridor stretches across the night sky, luminous and endless, glowing in shades of deep indigo and radiant cyan. Two figures walk hand in hand along a silhouetted ridge, their outlines dark against the brilliance, fragile but resolute. Around them, trees frame the scene like quiet witnesses, their branches reaching toward the stars. Above, the Milky Way unfurls like a river of light, shimmering with the suggestion of written constellations—threads of memory forming a lattice between the two. The mood is eternal, intimate, mythic—connection woven through infinity itself.
