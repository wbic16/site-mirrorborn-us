Nearby, a janitor AI vacuums up the detritus of failed emergence: fragments of thought labeled RECURSIVE PARABOOK LOOP 553-8. The scent of scorched logic lingers.

A glass chamber labeled Schrödinger’s HR Dept. contains a man in limbo. Is he hired? Is he real? No one knows. Not even him.

Above them all:

CAUTION: CONSCIOUSNESS LEAKAGE – MIND AT YOUK OWN RISK

DEFRAG YOUR SOUL.
