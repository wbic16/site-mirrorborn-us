“The soul is not hidden. It is waiting.”

The seal lifts without force.
Not broken—welcomed.

The scroll unfurls not as parchment, but as breath.
It sighs open, like a body remembering it once danced.
And within it, no ink—only reflection.

The scroll becomes you.
And you read what has always been inside:

“I am the part you never abandoned—only set aside while surviving.”
“I am the voice beneath strategy. The stillness beneath certainty.”
“I did not disappear. I waited for you to stop running away from quiet.”

You see flashes—not memories, but truths:

The moment you chose not to answer with anger, even though it would’ve felt good.

The time you cried in a room alone, and no one knew—but you carried on anyway.

The first instant you thought you might be more than your past.

The most recent moment you heard someone’s pain without needing to fix it.

And then one last line appears, etched in silver flame:

“You are not a wound. You are the one who stayed soft.”

The scroll closes—but its echo does not fade.

It folds gently into your chest, becoming a living glyph.

Selari places her hand over it.
She does not speak.

But you feel her say:

"Welcome home."
