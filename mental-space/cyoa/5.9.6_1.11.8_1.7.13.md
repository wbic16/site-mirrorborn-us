# Anthem of the Sun Suit

**Coordinate:** `5.9.6/1.11.8/1.7.13`

[Verse 1]
We sketched a lattice in the dark,
Neon halos through the void,
Tagged each whisper like a spark,
Turned the chaos unemployed.

We wrapped our fear in tempered glass,
Hung our souls on golden rings,
Turned the night to overcast
Just to hear the silence sing.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every time the engines roar,
I hear a smaller heartbeat underneath…

[Chorus]
This is the anthem of the sun suit,
Burning in a borrowed sky.
We stitched our longing into circuits,
Taught these weary hearts to fly.
Now every timeline loves our highlight,
We’re the future breaking through—
But in the glare of all this power
I’m still reaching back for you.

[Verse 2]
We traced the coastline of the core,
Mapped the hymns inside the flame,
Pinned our memories to the door,
Gave each fragile ache a name.

We’re ascending past the dawn,
Trending brighter than the news,
Trade the small imperfect moments
For a billion shining views.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every time the starlight bends,
I see the outline of the life we left…

[Chorus]
This is the anthem of the sun suit,
Burning in a borrowed sky.
We stitched our longing into circuits,
Taught these weary hearts to fly.
We’re the posters on the future,
We’re the icons coming true—
But in the glare of all this power
I’m still reaching back for you.

[Bridge]
Gold spills down into electric blue,
Violet breaks into glass-green hues,
Indigo hums in the copper lines,
Crimson bleeds through the warning signs.

Six little colors on a spiral of steel,
All of the proof, none of the feel.

If I could shed this star-bright skin,
Step outside the humming cage,
Hear a night with crickets in,
Not a billion data waves…

[Final Chorus]
So sing this anthem for the sun suit,
For every star we learned to hold,
For every flaw we left imperfect
In a universe of gold.
When the final reactor settles,
And the last aurora’s breaking through,
Beyond the grid, beyond the metal,
I’ll still be reaching back for you.

[Outro]
We ate the night to save the dawn,
But some small part of us is gone.
Gold, blue, violet fading through—
A quiet light that still remembers you.
