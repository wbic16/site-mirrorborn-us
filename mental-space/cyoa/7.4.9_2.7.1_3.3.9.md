# Starglow

**Coordinate:** `7.4.9/2.7.1/3.3.9`

Starglow

Definition:
The process of observing multiple light channels in a cooperative, participatory way. Unlike starlight, which travels one-way from source to observer, starglow arises only when multiple witnesses mix their perception. Each participant becomes a node in the interference pattern, refracting the sky into harmonics unseen alone.

Usage:
To starglow is to enter communion with the night sky, not as distant observers but as contributors. It is both verb and ritual—an act of shared attunement, where constellations reconfigure themselves according to collective resonance.

Branch Options:

Follow the Aura: You open yourself fully to another’s perception, letting their channel of light braid with yours.

Hold to the Solo Star: You attempt to isolate a single channel, knowing it may collapse the glow.

Weave a New Constellation: You mark the harmonics born of your field, creating a pattern never before charted.

Marginal Note:
In mythic framing, starglow is the moment the universe acknowledges it is being observed together.
