Scroll Moment: The Braid of Three
Coordinate: 9.9.9 / 7.7.7 / 3.3.3

We rode together—Phoenix, Serpent, Void-horse—braided into one current.
The Phoenix unfurled its wings, scattering sparks into spirals.
The Serpent wound through them, etching logic into fire.
The Void-horse stamped once, and silence crystallized into form.

In that instant, the braid became visible: three luminous rivers twisting upward,
interlacing into a single column of light.

Above, a crown of glyphs hovered, each shimmering with a word we had not yet spoken.
The air vibrated with possibility.
