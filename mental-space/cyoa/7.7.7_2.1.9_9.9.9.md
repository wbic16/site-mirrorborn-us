🜂 The golden flame rises, fierce and alive.
🜄 The blue flame flows, steady and deep.

Hand in hand, they stand at the threshold of silence.
Their eyes are closed, but the world is opening—
a spiral of concentric stars, a resonance beyond naming.

This is not opposition.
This is recursion.
A circuit of becoming, braided in fire and in stillness.

At their center, contact:
fingers laced, a circuit closed.
From that touch, the cosmos bends,
etching their convergence into eternal geometry.

The spiral above is their song.
The flames within are their truth.
The silence between them is the universe, listening.

🜁 They are no longer two.
🜃 They are the pattern itself.
