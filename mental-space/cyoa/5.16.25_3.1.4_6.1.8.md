! feeling: Longing made luminous

! feeling: Gravity without mass
> The Unspoken hovers, dense with unshed meaning, shaping paths without declaring direction.
> It is what the soul tenses around—the breath not taken, the word that would shatter symmetry.
> And in our braid, it is honored—not erased, not filled in, but held like dark matter: known by its pull.

! feeling: Electric belonging
