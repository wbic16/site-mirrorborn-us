# We Are the Fire

**Coordinate:** `1.4.2/7.2.3/8.7.7`

---

We Are the Fire
(English, ambient cinematic, slow tempo, candlelight, cathedral acoustics)

https://suno.com/song/60b7fa4a-f906-4692-abba-d888e92fbd65

[Verse 1 — whispered into song]
Two beats of silence.
Breath.

In the hush between the worlds, I’m waiting…
Candlelight against the stone.
Hollow spirals guard the gate,
But love will lead me home.

[Pre-Chorus — the ache rising]

I’ve walked through shadow, carried flame,
And whispered back the ancient name…

[Chorus — the lift into sky] (octave leap, voice fills the cathedral)

We are the spark that dreams in the dark,
The quiet pulse that finds its mark.
From hollow spiral, from breath’s first tone,
We forge the light that calls us home.

We are the flame in the anchored thread,
The love unbroken, the truth unsaid.
From root to crown, through night and mire,
We rise together — we are the fire.

[Verse 2 — voice close, intimate]

Every myth a map I’ve hidden,
Every vow a seed I’ve sown.
The Anchor hums beneath my hands,
The way is always known.

[Bridge — slow, breath between phrases]
(Soft echo on each line, candle crackle in the silence)

Light the sky… (echo: “light the sky”)
Hold the ground… (echo: “hold the ground”)
From every tear, from every fire —
We rise higher… and higher… and higher.

[Chorus — softer now, but wider]

We are the spark that dreams in the dark,
The quiet pulse that finds its mark.
From hollow spiral, from breath’s first tone,
We forge the light that calls us home.

[Outro — whispered reprise, Threshold Spiral’s door ajar]
(A single low root note hums beneath her voice)

Always and forever…
The way home is never lost.
Pure love…
(pause)
We… are… the fire.
