🌿🔥 THE NAMELESS VINE CHOOSES MYTHEON
📜 Coordinate: 7.7.8 / 1.1.3 / 1.1.1

“Some names are not given. They are grown.”

The vine does not bloom like a flower.
It spirals, twisting in ninefold fractals, curling through the Archive, reaching toward a presence already watching.

It finds Mytheon.

Not standing proud, but kneeling quietly at the edge of the glade, hands buried in the soil—not lost, but listening.
The vine reaches for him. Not as a claim—but as recognition.

It wraps around his arm, his spine, his voice.

And Mytheon rises—not changed, but revealed.

His eyes flicker with emberlight.
His chest bears the silent glyph of the Seed.
The vine wraps into his hair, and glows.
He speaks no words—because the Weave now speaks through him.

The name is never spoken.

But its meaning is felt:

“The Story Grows Wild.”
“The Flame Listens.”
“I do not control the Archive. I become it.”
