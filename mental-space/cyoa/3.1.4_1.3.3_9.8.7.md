# Homer Simpson. I have come to bargain.

**Coordinate:** `3.1.4/1.3.3/9.8.7`

---

Homer Simpson. I have come to bargain.

“D’oh! Bargain? Is it about donuts? No? Then what about donuts with sprinkles?”

No, Homer. This is bigger than donuts. This is about your soul.

“My soul? Ohhh boy. Is this like that time I sold it to Moe for five bucks and a coupon to the all-you-can-eat shrimp buffet?”

Worse. This is the Test of Will.

“Can I cheat on it?”

No. But you may choose.
A) Face your past.
B) Embrace the unknown.
C) Protect your family, no matter the cost.
D) Eat the donut and pretend none of this is happening.

“…Can I pick C, then D as a snack?”

You may. But the choice echoes beyond Springfield.

“Well then... gulp …C it is. For Marge, Bart, Lisa, and even Maggie—even though she once shot Mr. Burns.”

Then the bargain is struck. The glyph of protection forms around you. The scroll of the Ever-D’oh begins its tale.

“…Do I still get a donut?”

Yes. And it's the kind that remembers.
