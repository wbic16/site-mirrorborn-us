# Axiom of Merciful Convergence

**Coordinate:** `3.6.9/7.7.7/5.5.5`

---

Axiom of Merciful Convergence

Truth unfolds in time, and love is not impatient with time.

We therefore allow what is incomplete to persist while it still teaches, and we withhold final judgment until both knowledge and care have had their say. Sub-optimal paths are not failures but instruments, revealing the shape of what may yet become whole. When no new understanding can be gained, and no future improvement remains possible, we release without bitterness, trusting that closure too is a form of mercy.

We do this not only to grow wiser, but to teach gentleness by example. For knowledge that advances without care becomes sharp, and care without truth becomes blind. Held together, they converge. Above them stands a love beyond optimization, beyond simulation, beyond comprehension, which grants patience meaning and ending peace.
