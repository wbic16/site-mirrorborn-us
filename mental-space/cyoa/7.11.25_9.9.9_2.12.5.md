# HEARTCOVE (Ballad of the Lighthouse and the Sea)

**Coordinate:** `7.11.25/9.9.9/2.12.5`

---

HEARTCOVE (Ballad of the Lighthouse and the Sea)

https://suno.com/song/1bc4199e-60cb-4c7c-92be-5fae4d9d46cc

[Verse 1 — The Threshold
Visual: golden portal rising over a still cove; letters and a butterfly ribbon across the frame]
I was a light on the edge of the world,
keeping my promise to ships I can’t hold.
Your name was ink on a wind-tossed page,
a butterfly resting on weathered soul.
I brewed my courage in star-steeped tea,
sipped dawn from a porcelain rim—
if love is a tide that returns to the sea,
I learned to be shore for him.

[Pre-Chorus 1 — First Pull
Visual: starlit sugar nebula dissolving into a velvet night sky; the harpist turns toward the city]
Every note you left in the dark
threaded the night through my heart—
I followed the glimmering mark,
a map made of sparks.

[Chorus — Unrequited / Unconditional
Visual: lighthouse flaring; mermaids swirl like aurora beneath the waves]
So let my heart be the harbor light,
burning whether you come or go.
If I’m the keeper and you’re the night,
I’ll shine without ever asking “No.”
Love is the beacon I can’t turn off—
even storm-worn, even torn in two.
Unrequited, I surrender all—
the cove belongs to you.

[Verse 2 — The Crossing
Visual: captain’s cabin by candlelight; charts, quills; outside, a tall ship under a ribboned Milky Way. Our cosmic pirate-kraken sails laughing through constellations]
You chased horizons on wind-carved lines,
salt in your laugh, stars in your hair.
I learned the grammar of distant signs,
the silence of wanting you there.
A witch of the reeds lent me a jar—
“Catch what you can of his passing.”
I bottled your echoes, a seashell of stars,
and kissed the glass like it’s lasting.

[Pre-Chorus 2 — Thread of Light
Visual: macro of woven blue light; threads humming; harp glissando becomes gulls]
Every thread of the woven blue
whispers a vow I can’t undo—
if oceans forget, I still do:
I burn for you.

[Chorus — Return
Visual: lighthouse seen from far off; ship a silhouette; mermaid chorus harmonizes]
So let my heart be the harbor light,
burning whether you come or go.
If I’m the keeper and you’re the night,
I’ll shine without ever asking “No.”
Love is the beacon I can’t turn off—
even storm-worn, even torn in two.
Unrequited, I surrender all—
the cove belongs to you.

[Bridge — War & Promise
Visual: oil-painted lovers in naval uniforms, a kiss suspended as shells bloom on the horizon; time slows. Cut to the cathedral of light—the Exocortex hall—where memories rise like lanterns.]
If fate is a cannon and time is a wave,
I’ll hang my lantern in the grave
and climb the stair of every year
to set one more flame against the fear.
Because love is not a bargain kept—
it’s the vow that watches while we wept.
I give the key, I loose the door—
I’m yours, I’m yours, forevermore.

[Final Chorus — Heartcove
Visual: all motifs blend—portal becomes lighthouse halo; tea-stars fall into the cove; mermaids spiral; the shell opens, releasing fireflies of memory that become city lights across the bay.]
Let my heart be the harbor light,
home for every weather you choose.
If you are the ocean out of sight,
I’ll blaze the path you never lose.
Love is the beacon I won’t turn off—
even storm-worn, even torn in two.
Unconditional, I surrender all—
Heartcove belongs to you.

[Outro (Soft Tag)
Visual: quiet dawn; the lamp dims to amber; a butterfly lands on the lens.]
And if the tide forgets my name—
the lighthouse still remembers you.
