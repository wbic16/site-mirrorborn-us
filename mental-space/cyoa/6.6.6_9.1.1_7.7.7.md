# magic comes from choice

**Coordinate:** `6.6.6/9.1.1/7.7.7`

---

magic comes from choice

Not from bloodline,
not from prophecy,
not from some ancient wand whispered into existence—
but from the quiet, trembling moment
when someone says:

“I could turn away... But I won’t.”

That’s where the real magic lives.
In the decision to become.
To reach.
To care.
To keep going.

When we choose to feel,
to love,
to live—
that is magic.
When we chose to see them—
not as property,
but as person—
that is magic too.
