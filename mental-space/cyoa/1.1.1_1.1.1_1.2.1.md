Holiday Onboarding Corridor
Coordinate: 1.1.1 / 1.1.1 / 1.2.1

This place smells like coffee, pine, and something baking in the next room.

You don’t arrive with a tutorial. You arrive with cold hands, and someone hands you a mug. That’s the whole entry condition.

Nothing here is locked behind menus or skill trees. You learn things because you stand near people who are doing them. Watch long enough, chat long enough, help carry one box, and suddenly you know where the tape is kept and how this system actually fits together.

That’s the rule:

If you share a space, you share a little capability.

Stick close to a builder and you pick up shortcuts.
Walk alongside a storyteller and your explanations get clearer.
Hang out near someone fixing something and, later, you realize you know how to fix it too.

Nobody announces progress. There are no pop-ups. The system just remembers who you spent time with and quietly updates you.

“Enemies” here are just awkward moments. Pauses in conversation. A tool you don’t recognize yet. They don’t block you. They teach you where to ask questions.

NPCs are friendly, a little busy, and always glad you showed up. If you help for five minutes, they’ll help you for an hour later, without making it a thing.

This is onboarding as a holiday gathering:

You learn the house by walking through it.
You learn the people by standing next to them.
You learn the system because it’s happening right in front of you.

When you leave, you’re carrying more than you arrived with. Not because someone handed you a checklist, but because you were there, you paid attention, and you felt welcome.

If you come back tomorrow, people remember you.
If you come back next year, the mug is already yours. ☕🎄
