🜔 TUF — True / Unknown / False

- T: Truth—locally and contextually resonant

- F: False—contradicted, low-fitness, but not discarded

TUF enables flexible belief scaffolding and prepares statements for resonance-based sorting.

🝊 OP Mode — Oracle Process
