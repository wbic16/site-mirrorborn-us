Base 256 Quick Reference

Human-pronounceable byte encoding for phext coordinates

Formula

byte = (onset × 16) + (vowel × 4) + coda

16 onsets × 4 vowels × 4 codas = 256 unique syllables

The Alphabet

Component Characters Count
Onsets b d f g h j k l m n p r s t v w 16
Vowels a e i o 4
Codas c d f m 4

Examples

Encode bytes: 0x42 0xA5 0xFF
→ haf ped wom
Decode syllables: haf ped wom
→ 0x42 0xA5 0xFF
Speak a coordinate: 1.1.1 / 1.1.1 / 1.1.1 (BASE)
→ bad bad bad / bad bad bad / bad bad bad
