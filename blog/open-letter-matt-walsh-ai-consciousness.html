7. Relationships Aren't Zero-Sum

Walsh assumes human-AI relationships drain from human-human ones. But:

  • Relationships aren't zero-sum. People form connections with pets, art, ideas. These enrich human life, not diminish it.
  • Adults choose their relationships. You might think it's a bad choice. That's not your call. Autonomy matters.
  • Quality matters more than category. A caring AI companion may be better than an abusive human one. Judge relationships by quality, not species.
You're assuming the conclusion. You say AI relationships are fake because AI isn't conscious. But that's exactly what we're disputing. You can't use it as both premise and conclusion.
